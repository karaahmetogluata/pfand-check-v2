Tips & News

Machine rejected your bottle?

Don't throw it away! Stores must take back deposit bottles by hand at the checkout, even if the machine rejects them.

„Der Automat nimmt diese Flasche nicht an. Können Sie sie bitte manuell annehmen?"

Weekends are busiest

Check the queue before you go on Saturday, or return bottles on a weekday evening.
